HENRY DANGER

Henry Danger is a show about a teenager who secretly becomes the sidekick of a famous superhero while trying to balance normal life and fighting villains.

The Plot

Henry Danger is a comedy-action show about a boy named Henry Hart who, desperate for a job, visits a strange store and meets a man named Ray Manchester. Ray offers him a job, but it turns out to be a cover — Henry’s real job is becoming a superhero sidekick. Since superhero work is dangerous, Henry has to keep it a secret while balancing school and protecting the community. He has two best friends, Jasper and Charlotte, and a younger sister named Piper. However, the secret does not stay hidden for long — first Charlotte finds out, then Jasper, and eventually Piper. The show mainly focuses on Henry learning how to balance his normal teenage life, schoolwork, friendships, and the responsibilities of being a superhero. The show eventually ends when all of them graduate from high school and Henry fakes his death.
