Introduction

Hello, reader(s)...Every drawing has a story, but not every story is easy to tell. Mine began with a single moment that changed the way I saw a blank page. In this blog, you'll discover what led me, Ibukun, to start drawing and why it has become one of my favourite hobbies. Now... let's begin.

Why did I start drawing?

When I was younger, my dad tried to introduce my sister and I to music. My sister got invested immediately. For me, I can't say the same.

I was so bored of it that when the lesson teacher came I just went to another side of the room to play with my dolls. At that point, my dad just started to focus on my sister in the music aspect.

But my sister became really good to the point that she could play 3 instruments at 10. Me? None. Obviously I was tired (and kind of jealous) of the attention she was getting so I started spending more of my time drawing. You can probably guess what happens in the end.

Why I like drawing

Drawing is one of my favourite hobbies because it is fun and easy to do.

Whenever I have free time, I like to draw because it keeps me busy and stops me from getting bored.

Drawing also helps me relax and makes me feel calm after a stressful day.

It allows me to use my imagination and create different pictures. I enjoy drawing because it is both interesting and enjoyable.
